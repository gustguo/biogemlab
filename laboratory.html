Grading

Gemological Certification Standards

Every finished diamond is assessed according to the standard 4C framework used across the global gemological industry.

Carat Weight

Precision weighing to 0.001 ct. Typical memorial diamond range: 0.3 – 2.0 ct. Larger sizes available on request.

Color Grade

Assessed against master stone set. Memorial diamonds typically range from near-colorless (G-H) to light color.

Clarity Grade

Microscopic examination at 10x magnification. Inclusions mapped and documented for transparency.

Cut Quality

Proportions, symmetry, and polish assessed. Standard cuts: round brilliant, princess, cushion. Custom available.

Protocols

Laboratory Operating Protocols

Clean-Room Standards

Production zones maintained at ISO 8 – ISO 6 cleanliness levels. Personnel wear full protective equipment. Air filtration and positive pressure prevent contamination.

  • HEPA filtration
  • Positive pressure airflow
  • Regular contamination monitoring

Sample Integrity

Chain-of-custody protocols ensure that carbon source materials are never mixed, mislabeled, or compromised. Every sample has a unique batch ID from intake to delivery.

  • Unique batch ID system
  • Segregated processing lanes
  • Cross-contamination prevention

Data Security

Production records, partner data, and customer information are stored on secured systems with access controls. No production data is shared outside the partner relationship.

  • Encrypted storage
  • Role-based access control
  • Audit trail logging
