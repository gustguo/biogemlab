Industry Honesty

Why we say no to ash — and what we do instead.

Cremation destroys the carbon. The math is simple: you need ~5 kg of ash to extract enough carbon for one diamond. A human body produces 2–3 kg. We are the only manufacturer honest enough to say this out loud.

About BioGem Lab

A Carbon Engineering Laboratory

We do not make jewelry. We make diamonds — from carbon extracted from biological materials. Hair. Pet fur. Flowers. Plants. Any organic carbon-bearing material.

Our 2012 national invention patent covers the complete bio-carbon-to-diamond pipeline. Proprietary purification protocols achieve 99.95%+ carbon purity before HPHT crystal synthesis. The result: gem-quality laboratory-grown diamonds with full provenance documentation.
