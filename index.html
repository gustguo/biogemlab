About BioGem Lab

A Carbon Engineering Laboratory

We do not make jewelry. We make diamonds — from carbon extracted from biological materials. Hair. Pet fur. Ashes. Flowers. Plants. Any organic carbon-bearing material.

Our 2012 national invention patent covers the complete bio-carbon-to-diamond pipeline. Proprietary purification protocols achieve 99.95%+ carbon purity before HPHT crystal synthesis. The result: gem-quality laboratory-grown diamonds with full provenance documentation.

Manufacturing Pipeline

Seven-Stage Carbon-to-Diamond Process

Each stage operates under documented protocols with in-process quality checkpoints. Full traceability from raw material to finished gem.

Technical specifications
01

Biological Material Intake

Carbon source reception, documentation, and chain-of-custody logging. Minimum sample: 0.3g hair.

02

Carbon Extraction

Proprietary thermal and chemical decomposition process. Patent-protected protocol (2012).

03

Carbon Purification

Multi-stage refinement to 99.95%+ purity. Real-time spectroscopic monitoring. Critical quality gate.

04

Graphitization

Amorphous carbon conversion to crystalline graphite. Temperature-controlled annealing in inert atmosphere.

05

HPHT Crystal Growth

5.5 GPa pressure, 1,450°C temperature, 35-50 days depending on target carat weight.

06

Diamond Processing

Laser cutting, precision polishing, facet engineering. Partner-specified cut styles accommodated.

07

Grading & Certification

4C gemological assessment. CCIC traceability QR code. Optional IGI certification. Partner-branded documentation.
