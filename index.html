Digital Chain-of-Custody

Three-Layer Sample Protection

Every diamond is backed by physical sample retention, digital traceability records, and independent gemological certification.

Physical Sample Retention

Triple sample protocol: production sample (≥90%), archive sample (sealed ≥10 years), and verification sample for independent re-testing.

Digital Traceability ID

Each production batch receives a unique digital traceability code linked to growth parameters, processing dates, and quality checkpoints. Scan to verify origin and chain-of-custody.

Independent Certification

Every diamond undergoes independent gemological evaluation. Gemological certificates document the Four Cs — Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat weight — by certified authorities.

CCIC Digital Traceability

BioGem Lab provides CCIC (China Certification & Inspection Group) digital traceability codes for every production batch, combined with our proprietary mobile verification system and complete video documentation of the entire process — from carbon extraction through final gemological evaluation.

Customers scan the traceability code to access the full production record including process photos, growth parameters, and video documentation. This multi-layer transparency system provides confidence in the origin and authenticity of every diamond — a level of verification that sets your brand apart in the memorial diamond market.
