04 Industry honesty

Why we say no to ash — and what we do instead.

What a body yields2–3 KG ASH
→
Usable carbon left in it≈0.3% · 6–9 G
→
VerdictTHE MATH FAILS

Cremation destroys the carbon. The 6–9 grams that remain are carbonate locked inside bone mineral — not extractable feedstock — and 67–91% of even that comes from the cremation furnace's fuel, not the body (Cambridge University, 2017). Any manufacturer promising a diamond from ashes alone is selling sentiment, not chemistry. We extract carbon from hair, fur, flowers and plants — materials where the carbon is still there to be purified. We are the only manufacturer honest enough to say this out loud, and we publish the analysis.

  • Cross-border mutual recognition
  • Industry standard alignment

What CCIC verification actually means: China Certification & Inspection Group is a national-level inspection body, independent from any manufacturer — including us. Each certificate carries a unique QR code that resolves to the stone's chain-of-custody timeline: intake date, purification gate result, growth chamber, grading record. Your clients can verify their diamond's provenance themselves, in seconds, without contacting you or us. For partners selling into markets where memorial diamond provenance is contested, that independence is the difference between a claim and evidence.

Q.01Do you make memorial diamonds from ashes?

No — and no manufacturer honestly can. Cremation destroys the usable carbon: a body yields 2–3 kg of ash holding only ~0.3% carbon (6–9 grams), locked in bone-mineral carbonate — and 67–91% of even that trace derives from the furnace fuel, not the body. We extract carbon from hair, pet fur, flowers, and plants instead, where the carbon is intact, and we publish the full analysis behind this position.

Q.02How long does production take?

Around 60 days end to end. Crystal growth alone runs 18–25 days at 5.5 GPa and 1,450 °C inside our HPHT presses, preceded by patented carbon extraction and purification to 99.95%+, and followed by laser cutting, polishing, and 4C grading with CCIC certification.

Q.03What certification comes with each diamond?

Every stone ships with a CCIC national traceability certificate as standard — independently issued by China Certification & Inspection Group, with a unique QR code resolving to the full chain-of-custody timeline. An ILDA provenance upgrade is available for partners who need cross-border, mutually recognized dual attestation.

Q.04What sizes and grades do you manufacture?

Standard carats are 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, and 2.0 ct, graded colorless E–H only, VS clarity, Ideal cut, grown by HPHT synthesis. We deliberately do not produce colored diamonds — one grade band, one standard, no exceptions per batch. Custom sizes are available on request.

Q.05Do you ship under our brand?

Yes — full white-label. Production, certification, packaging, and documentation carry your brand name; customs documentation is included. Express delivery runs 3–7 days to North America, 3–5 days to Europe, and 2–8 days across Asia-Pacific, insured and tracked.

Q.06Is there a minimum order or capacity limit?

Current capacity is 500 ct per month across multi-chamber HPHT systems with a confirmed scaling roadmap. Minimum order quantities and volume timelines are structured per partner and detailed in your custom proposal — regional distributors can also negotiate exclusive territory.
