1. Service Description

BioGem Lab provides white-label and OEM memorial diamond manufacturing services. We transform biological carbon sources (hair, fur, nails, botanical material) into laboratory-grown diamonds using HPHT synthesis technology. All production is conducted at our laboratory facility in Luoyang, Henan, China.

Our services include:

  • Carbon purification and graphitization from biological sources
  • HPHT diamond synthesis and growth
  • Gemological cutting and polishing
  • Third-party certification (IGI or equivalent) — optional, at partner's request
  • White-label packaging and documentation under partner's brand
  • Global shipping to partner or partner's designated recipient

2. B2B Only — No Direct Consumer Sales

BioGem Lab operates exclusively as a B2B manufacturer. We do not sell directly to end consumers. All orders must be placed by authorized business partners, retailers, veterinary clinics, pet cremation services, or memorial companies. Partners are responsible for their own consumer-facing pricing, marketing, and customer service.

3. Minimum Orders and Pricing

We accept orders for single-unit samples and bulk production. Wholesale pricing is provided upon partnership inquiry and is subject to order volume, diamond specifications, and customization requirements. All pricing is quoted in USD unless otherwise agreed.

  • Sample orders: Single-unit pricing for evaluation purposes
  • Wholesale orders: Volume-based pricing for ongoing supply
  • OEM/ODM: Custom branding and packaging available

4. Production Timeline

Our standard production cycle is approximately 60 days from carbon receipt to finished gem. Timeline includes:

  • Carbon purification and graphitization: ~10 days
  • HPHT synthesis: ~10-20 days
  • Cutting and polishing: ~5-10 days
  • Certification (if requested): ~10-15 days
  • Shipping: ~5-10 days depending on destination

Timelines are estimates and may vary based on order specifications, laboratory capacity, and shipping logistics. We will communicate any delays to partners promptly.

5. Quality and Certification

All memorial diamonds are genuine laboratory-grown diamonds with the same physical, chemical, and optical properties as natural diamonds. Optional third-party certification (IGI) is available at partner's request and additional cost. We guarantee the carbon source used in production is derived from the biological material provided by the partner or their customer.

We provide all necessary documentation for customs clearance. Diamond products are non-dangerous goods and do not require special handling permits in most jurisdictions.
