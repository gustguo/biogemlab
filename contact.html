Common Questions

How long does partnership onboarding take?

From initial call to first live order: typically 2–4 weeks. Sample production takes ~60 days.

Do you require minimum volume commitments?

No hard minimums for standard white-label partnerships. Volume discounts begin at 50 ct/year.

Can I visit the facility?

Virtual facility tours are available for qualified partners. On-site visits require advance scheduling.

What's included in the sample evaluation?

A single memorial diamond with full documentation: certificate, traceability code, and production video access.
