Getting Started

What is the minimum order for a B2B partnership?

There is no minimum order. We operate on a manufacture-on-demand basis. You pay only when your customer places an order. Start with a single pilot production to validate quality and branding before scaling.

How long does it take to become a partner?

Typical timeline is 2–4 weeks from first contact to live production. Discovery call (15 min) → custom proposal (48 hrs) → pilot order (1 week production) → go live. Volume partners with existing infrastructure can go live in under 2 weeks.

Do I need any special equipment or lab space?

No. We are a full OEM/ODM manufacturer. You handle customer acquisition and sales; we handle everything else — carbon extraction, purification, synthesis, cutting, certification, and shipping. You never need to touch a lab machine or hold inventory.

What types of businesses do you partner with?

Pet cremation services, veterinary clinics (especially in-home euthanasia practices), pet memorial boutiques, jewelry brands expanding into memorial offerings, and funeral homes serving the pet market. We also work with luxury goods brands and cultural heritage organizations for commemorative collections.

Pricing & MOQ

How is wholesale pricing structured?

We use a tiered volume model. Entry tier (0–50 ct/yr) at standard wholesale rates. Growth tier (50–200 ct/yr) unlocks volume discounts and priority scheduling. Enterprise tier (200+ ct/yr) includes dedicated production allocation, territory exclusivity, and custom development.

Exact pricing depends on carat weight, cut complexity, and certification level. A 0.5ct diamond typically wholesales around $1,400 and retails for $2,800–$3,500. A 1.0ct diamond wholesales around $3,200 and retails for $6,500–$8,000. See the full pricing guide.

Are there setup fees or hidden charges?

No setup fees. No hidden charges. You only pay for the diamonds you order. Custom packaging design and branded certificate templates are included for Growth and Enterprise partners. Entry partners receive standard white-label documentation at no extra cost.

What payment terms do you offer?

Standard terms: 50% deposit on order, 50% balance before shipping. For established partners (6+ months, 10+ orders), net-30 terms are available. Enterprise partners may negotiate custom payment schedules. We accept wire transfer, credit card, and PayPal.

Do you offer volume rebates or annual discounts?

Yes. Enterprise partners (200+ ct/yr) receive a quarterly rebate based on total volume. Growth partners receive an annual loyalty discount starting from year two. We also run seasonal promotions for partners launching memorial diamond programs during peak pet loss seasons (October–January).

Production & Customization

What is the typical production timeline?

Standard production is approximately 60 days from carbon receipt to finished diamond. Breakdown: carbon purification (3–5 days), graphitization (2–3 days), HPHT synthesis (18–25 days), cooling and extraction (2–3 days), cutting and polishing (7–10 days), certification and packaging (3–5 days). Rush orders available for qualified partners at 15% surcharge.

Can I customize diamond specifications?

Yes. Standard options: round brilliant, princess, cushion, oval cuts in 0.25ct to 2.0ct. Color range: D–J. Clarity: VVS1–SI2. Custom cuts and fancy colors (blue, yellow, pink) available for Enterprise partners with 60+ day lead time. View our full capabilities.

What happens if a batch fails quality control?

Our batch success rate is 98.7% across 1,247+ completed cycles. In the rare event of a production failure, we restart the synthesis at no additional cost. If the failure is due to insufficient carbon purity from the sample, we contact the partner immediately with options: re-purify (adds 5–7 days) or request additional sample material. We never ship substandard diamonds.

How does white-label branding work?

Every diamond ships with: (1) Gemological certificate with your logo and certificate number, (2) Branded packaging with your design (or standard white-label box), (3) Traceability QR code linking to a verification page branded with your colors/logo, (4) Production documentation video. Your customer never sees BioGem Lab's name. We can also integrate with your existing fulfillment system for seamless dropshipping.

Shipping & Sample Collection

How much hair or fur is needed?

For pet memorial diamonds: approximately 6 grams of hair or fur per 1.0ct diamond. This is a conservative estimate with safety margin built in. Lower carat weights require proportionally less: ~3g for 0.5ct, ~1.5g for 0.25ct. If the sample is insufficient, we notify the partner immediately and can either pause production or proceed with a smaller diamond.

Can you work with cremated remains (ashes)?

Yes, but with important caveats. We primarily recommend hair/fur because it is easier to transport legally, has lower carbon impurity, and avoids the emotional complexity of handling ashes. If a partner specifically requests ash-based production, we require a larger sample (10–15g) and additional purification steps. Ash-based production adds 5–7 days to the timeline. We always advise partners to offer hair/fur as the primary option.

Is shipping carbon samples internationally legal?

Yes. Pet hair and fur samples are classified as non-dangerous goods (non-DG) for international shipping. No USDA permit is required for pet hair/fur entering most countries. We provide detailed shipping instructions and customs documentation for every partner. For cremated remains, regulations vary by country — we advise partners to check local requirements, though most jurisdictions allow small quantities for memorial purposes.

How do you ship finished diamonds to end customers?

We ship via FedEx or DHL Express with full insurance and tracking. For white-label partners, we use your branded packaging and include your return address. The customer receives a package that appears to come directly from your company. We can also ship to your warehouse if you prefer to handle final fulfillment. Average shipping time: 3–5 business days to US/EU, 5–7 days to Australia/NZ.

Do you offer direct-to-customer dropshipping?

Yes. This is our standard model for white-label partners. You sell the diamond to your customer at retail price. We manufacture and ship directly to them at wholesale cost. You never touch inventory. The customer sees only your brand on the package, certificate, and documentation. We can integrate with your e-commerce platform for automated order routing.

Quality & Guarantees

What certifications do you provide?

Every diamond includes a 4C gemological report (color, clarity, carat, cut) from an accredited laboratory. IGI certification is standard. GIA certification available on request for an additional fee. Enterprise partners may negotiate custom certification partnerships. All certificates are branded with your logo and certificate number.

What is your quality guarantee?

We guarantee every diamond meets the specifications ordered. If a diamond fails to meet the agreed color, clarity, or carat weight, we remake it at no charge. If a partner is unsatisfied with a pilot order, we offer a full refund of the wholesale cost. This guarantee applies to pilot orders and the first 3 production orders for new partners.

How do you ensure carbon traceability?

Every batch receives a unique Life-ID from the moment carbon enters our facility. This ID tracks the sample through purification, graphitization, synthesis, cutting, and certification. The partner and end customer can scan the QR code on the finished diamond to view the complete chain-of-custody documentation. This is powered by our partnership with CCIC (China Certification & Inspection Group) for national traceability standards.

What is your policy on remake or refund requests?

For production defects: immediate remake at no cost. For end-customer requests (e.g., size change, setting upgrade): partners handle customer service; we provide discounted remake pricing (typically 50% of wholesale). We do not offer cash refunds on completed diamonds, but we will remake or provide credit for production errors verified by our QC team.
