Challenge

Invisible Supply Chain

A prestigious Chinese cultural heritage brand required a 512-unit commemorative diamond collection to mark 70 years of continuous operation. The strict requirement: zero BioGem Lab branding on any customer-facing material.

Every certificate, box, card, and document had to appear as if produced entirely by the partner. This demanded a complete white-label infrastructure: custom velvet-lined presentation cases, gold-embossed partner logos, bilingual certificates, and RFID-enabled authenticity cards.

Solution

Turnkey OEM Pipeline

BioGem Lab deployed its full OEM manufacturing pipeline: carbon extraction from red sorghum grains, HPHT synthesis at 5.5 GPa / 1,450°C, precision cutting to VS1+ clarity and G+ color standards, and complete fulfillment packaging.

Round brilliant cut exclusively. Custom velvet-lined cases with gold-embossed partner logos. Bilingual certificates (Chinese/English). RFID-enabled authenticity cards for each unit. Direct-to-customer dropshipping under the partner's brand identity.

Result

512 Units, 0.3% Return

All 512 diamonds were delivered over 18 months with an average fulfillment time of 48 days. The return rate was just 0.3% — well below industry standards for commemorative jewelry.

The partner successfully launched a limited-edition heritage collection that sold through allocated channels. BioGem Lab remained entirely invisible to end consumers, validating the white-label model at scale.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can agricultural byproducts be used as carbon sources for memorial diamonds?

Yes. The Moutai Diamond project demonstrated that sorghum grains, grain husks, and other agricultural byproducts can be successfully converted into gem-quality diamonds through our patented carbon extraction and HPHT synthesis process. Any carbon-bearing organic material can be processed.

What does "zero BioGem Lab branding" mean in practice?

It means every customer-facing element — certificates, packaging, documentation, shipping labels, and traceability portals — carries only the partner's brand identity. BioGem Lab operates as a silent manufacturer. Even the CCIC traceability QR code resolves to a page branded with the partner's logo and colors.

What quality standards were maintained for this production run?

All 512 diamonds met VS1+ clarity and G+ color standards. Round brilliant cut exclusively. Each unit underwent 4C gemological assessment and received an independent grading report. The 0.3% return rate indicates consistent quality across the full production run.

How long does a 500-unit OEM contract typically take?

The Moutai Diamond contract was executed over 18 months with an average per-unit fulfillment of 48 days. Larger contracts can be staged with quarterly delivery schedules to align with partner marketing calendars and inventory needs.

What packaging options are available for white-label partners?

Partners can specify custom packaging from velvet-lined presentation cases to minimalist eco-friendly boxes. Gold embossing, laser etching, partner logos, and bespoke color schemes are all supported. RFID-enabled authenticity cards can be included as an optional upgrade.
