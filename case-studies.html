Silmaril — Equine Memorial Diamonds

A leading memorial diamond brand in the Asia-Pacific region, Silmaril serves the equestrian community with horse-hair memorial diamonds. BioGem Lab provides custom production for Silmaril's Guardian collection, with a typical turnaround of approximately 50 days from sample receipt to partner-branded delivery.

Silmaril's customers receive fully white-labeled products: partner-branded gemological certificates, custom packaging with the Silmaril crest, and a dedicated traceability portal where clients enter their unique Life-ID to view production photographs and carbon extraction documentation.
