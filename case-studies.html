Vetting Process

Our Partner Selection Criteria

Not every business is a fit. Here's how we evaluate potential partnerships.

Market Alignment

Partners must serve a market where memorial diamonds add genuine value. Pet cremation, veterinary aftercare, and jewelry retail are natural fits. We do not partner with businesses whose primary revenue model conflicts with memorial products.

Brand Integrity

Your brand reputation matters. We conduct due diligence on partner business practices, customer reviews, and market standing. White-label production means our quality becomes your reputation.

Volume Commitment

Minimum viable partnership requires capacity to process 10+ orders per quarter. This ensures the onboarding investment — custom documentation, packaging design, and workflow integration — is economically justified.

Geographic Feasibility

We ship globally but optimize for regions where customs clearance for synthetic diamonds is well-documented. Partners in markets with restrictive import policies may require additional compliance review.

Quality Assurance Framework

Every diamond passes through a 12-point inspection protocol before leaving our laboratory.

Stage Inspection Standard
Carbon Receipt Visual inspection + weight verification ≥95% biogenic carbon purity
Graphitization XRD crystallinity analysis ≥98% graphite phase
HPHT Growth In-situ temperature/pressure monitoring 1,400–1,600°C, 5–6 GPa
Post-Growth Optical microscopy + Raman spectroscopy No inclusions >50μm, 1,332 cm⁻¹ peak
Cutting Proportion analysis (Sarin scan) Table 53–58%, depth 60–62.5%
Final Certification IGI independent grading + CCIC traceability Color, clarity, carat, cut documented
