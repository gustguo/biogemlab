A leading memorial diamond brand in the Asia-Pacific region, Silmaril serves the equestrian community with horse-hair memorial diamonds. BioGem Lab provides custom production for Silmaril's Guardian collection, with a typical turnaround of approximately 50 days from sample receipt to partner-branded delivery.

Silmaril's customers receive fully white-labeled products: partner-branded gemological certificates, custom packaging with the Silmaril crest, and a dedicated traceability portal where clients enter their unique Life-ID to view production photographs and carbon extraction documentation.

Specialty · Inner Mongolia

Camel Wool — Nomadic Heritage

A cultural preservation project converting camel wool from Inner Mongolian herders into commemorative diamonds. Each stone represents a family's pastoral heritage and connection to the land.

Carbon extraction from animal fiber requires specialized purification to remove keratin impurities. BioGem Lab's patented process achieves ≥99.5% carbon purity from camel wool. Custom Mongolian-themed packaging with traditional knot motifs.

Animal fiber Cultural heritage
Heritage · Taiwan

Millennium Camphor — Rare Wood Heritage

A limited-edition commemorative diamond collection using carbon extracted from centuries-old camphor wood. Sourced from protected heritage trees in Taiwan, each diamond carries a unique wood-grain pattern preserved in the carbon structure.

Due to the density and resin content of ancient wood, this project required 18 months of R&D to optimize the graphitization protocol. Only 36 diamonds produced annually. Each unit includes a wood-origin certificate and tree-ring age documentation.

Ancient wood Limited edition
Consumer · China

Baby First Hair — Newborn Keepsake

A direct-to-consumer service converting infant first hair into petite memorial diamonds. Popular as a milestone gift for new parents, typically set in small pendants or rings.

Due to the minimal carbon yield from baby hair (typically 0.1–0.3g), this service specializes in sub-0.25ct stones with delicate cuts. Soft pink or blue-themed packaging options. 500+ families served since 2022.
