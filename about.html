About BioGem Lab

We Make Diamonds.
You Build the Brand.

BioGem Lab is a carbon engineering laboratory. We manufacture memorial diamonds — from biological carbon, at laboratory precision — for partners who serve the end customer.

Who We Are

One Problem. Twenty-Three Years.

Since 2003 we have worked on one problem: turning the carbon in living things — hair, fur, plant fiber — into gem-quality diamonds that are chemically and optically identical to mined stones.

We manufacture. Our partners brand, sell, and own the customer relationship. That is the entire model: your brand, our science, zero inventory risk on your side.

From biological carbon intake to a certified, white-label diamond in 60 days — 1,247+ batches completed for partners across 14 countries.

History

From Research to Production

2003

Carbon Extraction R&D Begins

Li Lihua and Wang Hongtao begin collaborative research into extracting carbon from biological sources for diamond synthesis.

2010

Core Patent Filed

Application filed for ZL 2010 1 0565778.9 — "A Method for Extracting Carbon from Biological Raw Materials for Diamond Production."

2012

National Patent Granted

Certificate No. 1058820 issued. The foundational intellectual property for bio-carbon extraction in memorial diamond manufacturing.

2021

Company Incorporated

Operational entity incorporated for international B2B manufacturing.
