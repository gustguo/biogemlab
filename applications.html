A 5.33-carat heritage diamond created for the 33rd World Hakka Conference, synthesizing carbon from ancient Guanlin cypress roots, historic city soil, and Yellow River water. Demonstrates institutional-scale commemorative capability.

  • CCIC traceability + cultural institution endorsement
