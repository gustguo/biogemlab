FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for B2B partners, memorial brands, and industry professionals about our manufacturing process, technology, and partnership models.

Manufacturing Process

01 How are memorial diamonds manufactured?

Memorial diamonds are created through High-Pressure High-Temperature (HPHT) synthesis. The process involves: (1) Carbon extraction from biological materials, (2) Purification to 99.95%+ purity, (3) Conversion to graphite, (4) HPHT crystal growth at 5.5 GPa and 1,450°C, (5) Cutting and polishing, (6) Grading and certification. The entire cycle takes approximately 60 days.

02 What is HPHT diamond synthesis?

HPHT (High-Pressure High-Temperature) synthesis replicates the natural conditions deep within the Earth's mantle where diamonds form. A carbon source is placed in a growth chamber and subjected to extreme pressure (5.5 GPa, equivalent to 150km underground) and temperature (1,450°C), causing carbon atoms to crystallize into diamond lattice structures over 40-80 days depending on desired carat weight.

03 What biological materials contain usable carbon?

We can extract usable carbon from: human hair, pet fur, nails, flowers, plants, feathers, wool, and other organic carbon-bearing materials. All mammalian keratin-based materials (hair, fur, wool) are highly compatible. We do not process cremated ashes, as the carbon content is insufficient for diamond synthesis. Minimum sample requirements vary by material type and desired carat weight — for a 1.0ct memorial diamond, approximately 5–10 grams of clean, dry fur or hair is required. We'll provide a collection kit with instructions.

04 Are these real diamonds?

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds are physically, chemically, and optically identical to mined diamonds. The only difference is origin — one grew in the earth over millions of years, one grew in our lab over approximately 60 days. A gemologist cannot distinguish between them without specialized equipment. They test as real diamonds on standard diamond testers and are graded using the same 4C criteria (carat, color, clarity, cut) as mined diamonds.

05 What colors and sizes are available?

We offer sizes from 0.3ct to 2.0ct (recommended from 0.5ct). Color options include colorless (D–E–F), near-colorless (G–H), and fancy colors (blue, yellow — limited availability). Most partners and families choose colorless or near-colorless. Cut shapes include round brilliant, princess, cushion, and emerald. Custom jewelry settings (rings, pendants, earrings) can be arranged through our partner jewelers.

06 What purity level is required for diamond synthesis?

Our carbon purification process achieves 99.95%+ purity before graphitization. This exceeds the threshold required for gem-quality diamond synthesis. Lower purity carbon can result in structural defects, reduced clarity, and color inconsistencies in the final diamond. Our proprietary purification protocol is protected under our 2012 national invention patent.

Samples & Certification

01 How do I send the fur or hair sample?

We provide collection kits with sterile vials, instructions, and prepaid return labels. Samples are shipped via FedEx or DHL with full tracking. Pet hair is classified as non-hazardous biological material for customs purposes, and we provide all necessary documentation for international transit. Typical sample transit time is 3–5 days. We recommend collecting clean, dry fur from a brush or during grooming — avoid samples contaminated with chemicals or dyes.

02 What certification options are available?

Every diamond comes with a gemological certificate. Our standard package includes a Chinese gemology laboratory certificate and a free CCIC digital traceability QR code linking to full production records. Optional international upgrades include IGI certificate (+$150) and GIA certificate (+$300). Most partners include the base certificate + CCIC code in their standard offering, and upsell IGI/GIA as premium options.

Partnership & Business

05 Can BioGem Lab support OEM partnerships?

Yes. We offer full white-label manufacturing where your brand appears on all packaging, certificates, and documentation. We ship directly to your end customer under your brand name. You maintain the customer relationship; we handle production, quality control, and logistics. No inventory investment required — manufacture-on-demand model.

06 What industries does BioGem Lab serve?

We serve: memorial diamond brands, pet cremation companies, veterinary clinics, jewelry manufacturers, funeral service groups, luxury memorial companies, and global distributors. We do not sell directly to consumers — all partnerships are strictly B2B.

07 What is the production timeline?

Standard production cycle is approximately 60 days from carbon sample receipt to finished diamond delivery. This includes: sample processing (3-5 days), purification (7-10 days), graphitization (5-7 days), HPHT growth (35-50 days depending on carat), cutting/polishing (5-7 days), and grading/certification (3-5 days). Express service available for qualified partners with volume commitments.

08 How does quality control work?

Our quality control system includes: in-process carbon purity spectroscopic analysis, growth chamber parameter monitoring, post-growth visual inspection, gemological grading (color, clarity, carat, cut), and final certification. Every diamond carries a unique CCIC traceability QR code for full chain-of-custody documentation. Partners receive complete production records including video documentation of key stages.

09 What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on partnership type and diamond specifications. For wholesale supply, we accommodate single-unit orders for testing and evaluation. For OEM white-label partnerships, we typically discuss volume commitments during partnership onboarding. Contact us with your projected volume for a tailored partnership proposal.

10 Do you provide international shipping?

Yes. We ship globally via FedEx and DHL with full tracking and customs documentation. For white-label partners, we ship directly to your end customer with your branded packaging and documentation. We work with trusted international logistics partners to ensure secure, insured delivery of finished diamonds.

11 Can the diamond be set into jewelry?

Yes. We work with partner jewelers who can set memorial diamonds into rings, pendants, earrings, bracelets, and other custom pieces. You can use your own jeweler if you prefer, or we can coordinate the setting directly. Typical jewelry turnaround adds 2–3 weeks to the overall production timeline. We recommend discussing setting options early in the process so the final design can be planned alongside diamond growth.

12 What happens if a customer needs to cancel or change their order?

If a cancellation is requested within 48 hours of sample receipt — before diamond growth begins — we refund the deposit minus a processing fee. Once HPHT synthesis has started, the process cannot be reversed because the carbon is already committed to the growth chamber. For carat size or color changes requested before growth begins, we can accommodate if the sample size is sufficient. We recommend confirming all specifications with the customer before submitting the sample to avoid changes.

13 What kind of partner support does BioGem Lab provide?

We provide full partner support including: branded collection kits with instructions and prepaid return labels, a white-label status tracking portal (embeddable on your website), product photography and process videos for marketing, FAQ copy and technical documentation, staff training via video call, and direct email support with 24-hour response time. We also offer a partner sample program — a small display diamond grown from synthetic carbon (not pet hair) for $200, fully refundable against your first real order.

14 Do you offer exclusive territories or market protection?

We do not offer exclusive territories — we are a production laboratory, not a franchise. However, we do not oversaturate markets. If we already have multiple active partners in a specific region, we will inform you honestly and discuss whether the market has capacity for additional partners. We prioritize quality partnerships over quantity, and we would rather have a small number of strong partners than many underperforming ones. Transparency about existing partner density is part of our onboarding process.
