Challenge

From Living Root to Permanent Gem

The 33rd World Hakka Conference sought a commemorative object that embodied the Hakka spirit of rootedness and migration — something permanent enough to outlast the conference itself, yet personal enough to represent ancestral connection.

A cypress tree root from a historic Hakka settlement was selected as the carbon source. The challenge: extracting sufficient pure carbon from wood root tissue — structurally dense and chemically complex — to produce a gem-quality diamond of significant size.

Solution

Patented Botanical Carbon Extraction

BioGem Lab applied its patented bio-carbon extraction process (ZL 2010 1 0565778.9) to break down the lignin and cellulose structure of the cypress root, isolating elemental carbon through controlled thermal decomposition and acid purification.

The extracted carbon was purified to 99.95%+ and graphitized before HPHT synthesis. After 18–25 days of growth at 5.5 GPa and 1,450°C, a 5.33ct rough crystal was formed — large enough for an emerald cut that maximised the stone's clarity and geometric presence.

Result

A Conference Heirloom

The finished 5.33ct emerald-cut diamond was presented at the 33rd World Hakka Conference as a permanent symbol of Hakka heritage — a piece of ancestral land transformed into a gem that will outlast generations.

The diamond was certified by CCIC with full traceability documentation, including the carbon source origin, extraction date, and synthesis parameters. It remains one of the largest botanical-source memorial diamonds produced by BioGem Lab.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you create diamonds from tree roots or other plant sources?

Yes. BioGem Lab's patented carbon extraction process can handle a wide range of biological sources, including tree roots, plant fibers, hair, and fur. The Hakka Ancestral Root Diamond project used cypress root — a dense, lignin-rich material that required extended extraction time but yielded sufficient pure carbon for a 5.33ct diamond.

What is the largest diamond you can produce from botanical carbon?

Diamond size depends on the carbon yield of the source material and the available HPHT synthesis capacity. The Hakka Ancestral Root Diamond at 5.33ct represents one of our largest botanical-source productions. For typical partner projects, standard SKUs range from 0.5ct to 2.0ct.

Do you provide documentation for cultural or heritage projects?

Yes. We provide full traceability documentation including carbon source origin, extraction photographs, synthesis parameters, and gemological certification. For heritage projects, we can also provide narrative documentation suitable for museum displays, conference presentations, or commemorative publications.

How long does botanical carbon extraction take compared to hair?

Botanical sources like wood and plant fiber typically require longer extraction than hair due to their cellulose and lignin structure. While hair extraction takes 7–10 days, dense wood roots may require 14–18 days. The total project timeline remains approximately 60 days from sample intake to finished diamond.
