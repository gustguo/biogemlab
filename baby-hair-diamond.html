Challenge

First Hair Forever

A prestigious Chinese cultural heritage brand required a [TBD]-unit commemorative diamond collection to mark 70 years of continuous operation. The strict requirement: zero BioGem Lab branding on any customer-facing material.

Baby hair is soft, fine, and contains less carbon per gram than adult hair. The extraction protocol required careful handling to maximize carbon yield from a limited sample size without damaging the original hair's sentimental integrity.

Solution

Gentle Hair Processing

BioGem Lab applied its patented hair carbon extraction protocol — originally developed for pet memorial diamonds — to baby hair. The fine strands required modified washing and purification to achieve ≥99.9% carbon purity from a limited sample.

The extracted carbon was synthesized via HPHT at 5.5 GPa / 1,450°C, cut to the parents' chosen specification, and delivered in a custom keepsake case with the original hair sample preserved alongside the diamond.

Result

[TBD] Units, 0.3% Return

All [TBD] diamonds were delivered over 18 months with an average fulfillment time of 48 days. The return rate was just 0.3% — well below industry standards for commemorative jewelry.

The original hair lock was preserved in a sealed display compartment. The family now has both the physical memento and the diamond — two forms of the same precious beginning.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can agricultural byproducts be used as carbon sources for memorial diamonds?

Yes. The Baby Hair Diamond project demonstrated that sorghum grains, grain husks, and other agricultural byproducts can be successfully converted into gem-quality diamonds through our patented carbon extraction and HPHT synthesis process. Any carbon-bearing organic material can be processed.

What does "zero BioGem Lab branding" mean in practice?

It means every customer-facing element — certificates, packaging, documentation, shipping labels, and traceability portals — carries only the partner's brand identity. BioGem Lab operates as a silent manufacturer. Even the CCIC traceability QR code resolves to a page branded with the partner's logo and colors.

What quality standards were maintained for this production run?

All [TBD] diamonds met VS1+ clarity and G+ color standards. Round brilliant cut exclusively. Each unit underwent 4C gemological assessment and received an independent grading report. The 0.3% return rate indicates consistent quality across the full production run.

How long does a 500-unit OEM contract typically take?

The Baby Hair Diamond contract was executed over 18 months with an average per-unit fulfillment of 48 days. Larger contracts can be staged with quarterly delivery schedules to align with partner marketing calendars and inventory needs.

What packaging options are available for white-label partners?

Partners can specify custom packaging from velvet-lined presentation cases to minimalist eco-friendly boxes. Gold embossing, laser etching, partner logos, and bespoke color schemes are all supported. RFID-enabled authenticity cards can be included as an optional upgrade.
