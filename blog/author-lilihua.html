About

Li Lihua is the Chief Scientist at BioGem Lab and the original inventor of the proprietary carbon extraction technology that converts biological materials — human hair, pet fur, plant fibers — into high-purity carbon suitable for HPHT diamond synthesis. This technology is protected under Chinese National Invention Patent ZL 201010565778.9, filed in 2010 and granted in 2012.

The patented process addresses a fundamental materials science problem: biological carbon sources contain nitrogen, sulfur, trace metals, and organic contaminants that must be removed to achieve the >99.95% carbon purity required for gem-quality diamond growth. Li's multi-stage thermal and chemical purification pipeline achieves this purity threshold consistently, enabling memorial diamond production at industrial scale.

The patent covers the complete carbon extraction pipeline: thermal carbonization of biological material, mixed-acid purification to remove contaminants, and the resulting high-purity carbon product suitable for graphitization and HPHT diamond synthesis. This intellectual property is the technical foundation of BioGem Lab's white-label memorial diamond manufacturing service.

Continuous R&D

Beyond the original 2010 patent, Li has led continuous process refinement at BioGem Lab's Luoyang facility. Key technical developments include:

  • Closed-loop chemical recovery systems that reduce reagent consumption and waste generation in carbon purification
  • Process standardization for batch-to-batch consistency in carbon purity and graphitization yield
  • Integration with automated HPHT press scheduling for optimized growth cycle timing
