Facility

Manufacturing Facility Architecture

Our production facility is purpose-built for memorial diamond synthesis, with segregated clean zones for each production stage. The layout follows a unidirectional material flow to prevent cross-contamination.

Intake & Receiving Zone

Sample logging, photography, and initial storage. Temperature-controlled environment.

Carbon Extraction Laboratory

Fume hoods, thermal processing stations, acid digestion infrastructure.

HPHT Synthesis Hall

Multi-chamber synthesis presses, thermal monitoring, pressure control systems.

Finishing & Grading Suite

Cutting, polishing, gemological assessment, packaging.

Clean-Room Floor

Equipment

Synthesis & Analysis Equipment

Belt-Type HPHT Presses

Primary synthesis equipment. Belt-type multi-anvil configuration capable of sustained 5.5 GPa pressure with precise thermal gradient control.

Cubic Multi-Anvil Presses

Secondary synthesis line for larger-diameter growth cells. Six-anvil cubic configuration for specialized batch requirements.

Spectroscopic Analyzers

Raman and FTIR spectroscopy for carbon purity verification, in-process quality monitoring, and final diamond characterization.

Thermal Analysis Systems

DSC and TGA instruments for carbon material characterization, graphitization monitoring, and thermal behavior assessment.

Diamond Processing Station

Complete cutting, bruting, and polishing infrastructure for round brilliant, princess, cushion, and custom cuts.

Gemological Grading Lab

In-house 4C assessment with reference master stones, digital microscopes, and precision weighing to 0.001 ct.

Quality Assurance

Multi-Stage Quality Control Protocol

In-Process Monitoring

1

Carbon Purity Checkpoint

Spectroscopic verification of ≥99.95% carbon purity before graphitization. Failed batches are reprocessed.

2

Graphitization Confirmation

XRD analysis to confirm crystalline graphite phase. Amorphous carbon content must be below threshold.

3

Growth Chamber Inspection

Pre- and post-growth chamber disassembly documentation. Seal integrity and catalyst condition recorded.

4

Mid-Cycle Parameter Audit

Automated logging of temperature, pressure, and power consumption. Anomaly detection triggers operator review.

Post-Production Verification

5

Rough Diamond Inspection

Visual and microscopic examination for cracks, inclusions, and color zoning before processing.

6

4C Grading Protocol

Independent gemological assessment by trained graders. Color, clarity, cut, and carat documented.

7

Traceability Assignment

CCIC traceability QR code linked to batch record. Full production documentation archived.

8

Final Packaging Audit

Partner-branded packaging verified against specifications. Certificate, video link, and care instructions included.
