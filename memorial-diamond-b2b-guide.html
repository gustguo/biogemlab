Quality Promise

What You Can Promise Your Customers

When you white-label our diamonds, these are the standards you can stand behind. Every stone that leaves our lab has passed through these checkpoints.

Real Diamond, Verified

Every diamond is independently graded by IGI for the 4Cs — color, clarity, cut, and carat. Your customers receive a certificate they can verify online.

Carbon Source Tracked

From the moment your customer's sample arrives at our lab, it gets a unique ID. Every step — extraction, growth, cutting — is logged and traceable via CCIC.

Six-Stage Inspection

Carbon purity check, growth monitoring, post-growth microscopy, cutting proportion analysis, and final certification. Six gates, no shortcuts.

Patent-Backed Process

Our carbon extraction technology is protected under CNIPA Patent ZL 2010 1 0565778.9. This isn't a black box — it's a documented, protected process you can reference.

Predictable Timeline

Standard production takes about 60 days from carbon receipt to finished diamond. You can give your customers a reliable delivery date — and meet it.

Failed Batch Policy

If a production batch doesn't meet our standards, we re-process at no charge. You never pay for a stone that fails inspection. Period.

Bottom line: 98.7% of production cycles clear all checkpoints on the first pass. The ones that don't get fixed on our dime. See how we manufacture.
