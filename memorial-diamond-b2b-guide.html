Inspection Protocol

Quality Control Protocols

Every diamond is subjected to multi-stage inspection before it leaves our facility. Your customers receive only stones that pass all checkpoints.

Stage Inspection Method Acceptance Threshold Rejection Rate
Carbon Receipt Visual inspection + gravimetric analysis ≥95% biogenic carbon purity; ≥0.3g net carbon yield 3.2%
Graphitization XRD crystallinity + SEM morphology ≥98% graphite phase; particle size 10–50μm 1.8%
HPHT Growth In-situ thermocouple + pressure transducer logging 1,400–1,600°C; 5.0–6.0 GPa; ≥48h stable hold 4.5%
Post-Growth Optical microscopy + Raman spectroscopy No inclusions >50μm; 1,332 cm⁻¹ peak FWHM ≤5 cm⁻¹ 2.1%
Cutting & Polishing Sarin proportion scan + Hearts & Arrows imaging Table 53–58%; depth 60–62.5%; symmetry ≥Good 1.4%
Final Certification IGI independent 4C grading + CCIC traceability audit Color E–H; clarity VS+; carat ±0.02ct; cut documented 0.7%

Overall Batch Success Rate: 98.7% of production cycles meet all six checkpoints without rejection. Failed batches are documented, analyzed, and re-processed at no additional cost to the partner. View full manufacturing process.
