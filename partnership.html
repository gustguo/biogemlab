Common Questions

Partnership FAQ

Answers to the questions partners ask before their first order. If you need specifics, your proposal call covers everything in detail.

What is the minimum order for a B2B partnership?

We offer flexible entry terms. Type A (pet aftercare) partners can start with consignment or wholesale. Type B (jewelry/gift) operates on wholesale buy-in. Type C (brand agent/OEM) typically starts with custom packaging at volume. Exact terms are provided in your custom proposal.

Can I customize the diamond specifications for my brand?

Yes. We offer cut, color, clarity, and carat weight customization. Popular options include round brilliant, princess, and cushion cuts in 0.25ct to 2.0ct. We also support custom cuts for premium partners. View our laboratory capabilities.

How does white-label packaging and documentation work?

Every shipment includes gemological certificates, branded packaging, and a traceability QR code — all under your logo. Your customer never sees BioGem Lab's name. We can also customize certificate layouts and packaging design for volume partners.

What is the typical production timeline for memorial diamonds?

Our standard production cycle is approximately 60 days from carbon receipt to finished diamond. This includes purification, graphitization, HPHT synthesis, cutting, and certification. Rush orders are available for qualified partners. See the full manufacturing timeline.

Do you offer direct-to-customer shipping?

Yes. We ship directly to your end customer under your brand name. You never need to hold inventory. We handle fulfillment, tracking, and customer notifications — all branded as your company.

Do you process cremation ashes?

No. We specialize in hair, pet fur, and botanical carbon sources only. We do not process cremated human remains due to regulatory complexity and our strategic focus on non-cremation memorial segments.

Do you offer rush production?

No rush service is available. Our 60-day target is already market-leading. Quality control will not be compromised for acceleration.

Your specific situation may not fit these general answers. The initial contact is where we discuss your market, volume, and brand requirements in detail.
